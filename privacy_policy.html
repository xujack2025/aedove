Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to AeDove. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application for local file transfer.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide

AeDove operates as a local file transfer application. We do NOT collect or store any personal information on external servers. However, the app may access:

  • Device Name: To identify your device on the local network for file transfers
  • Files and Media: Files you choose to send or receive through the app
  • Device Information: Basic device information for compatibility and functionality

2. Automatically Collected Information

  • Local Network Information: IP addresses on your local network for device discovery
  • Storage Permissions: Access to read and write files you choose to transfer
  • Location Permissions: On Android, required for Wi-Fi scanning and device discovery (not used to track your physical location)
Important: All file transfers happen directly between devices on your local network. We do NOT upload, store, or access your files on any external servers.

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for:

  1. Device Discovery: Finding other devices running AeDove on the same local network
  2. File Transfer: Enabling peer-to-peer file transfers between devices
  3. App Functionality: Ensuring the app operates correctly and efficiently
  4. Analytics: Understanding app usage patterns to improve functionality (if applicable)

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage Only

All file transfers are performed directly between devices on your local network. Files are temporarily stored in your device's local storage during transfer and are not uploaded to any cloud service or external server.

Security Measures

  • Peer-to-peer encryption for file transfers
  • Local network communication only
  • No external data transmission
  • Secure storage of files on your device

Permissions Required

AeDove requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • Storage/Photos and Media: To access files you want to send and save files you receive
  • Location (Android): Required by Android for Wi-Fi scanning and local network device discovery (not used to track your location)
  • Network Access: To discover devices and transfer files on your local network

Note: You can manage these permissions in your device settings. Denying certain permissions may limit app functionality.

Third-Party Services

Advertisements

AeDove may display advertisements to support the free version of the app. These ads are provided by third-party advertising networks and may collect certain information. Please refer to the privacy policies of our advertising partners:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services to understand app usage and improve functionality. These services may collect anonymous usage data.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share information
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party service providers (e.g., ad networks) who assist in operating the app

Children's Privacy

AeDove is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request access to the information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information (note: most data is stored locally on your device)
  • Opt-Out: Disable permissions or uninstall the app at any time

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over data retention. Files received through AeDove remain on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the app will not automatically delete received files from your device's storage.

International Users

AeDove is designed for local network file transfers and does not transmit data internationally. All file transfers occur within your local network regardless of your geographic location.
